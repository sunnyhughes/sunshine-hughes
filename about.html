About Me

I am a community-centered advocate, educator, and web developer committed to creating access, opportunity, and meaningful support for individuals and families.

Rooted in Experience, Guided by Purpose

My work is shaped by both professional training and lived experience. I understand firsthand how systems can feel overwhelming — and how powerful it can be when someone meets you with empathy, clarity, and practical guidance.

Over the years, I have supported individuals and families navigating recovery, parenting, court systems, workforce readiness, and personal growth. I believe in approaches that honor dignity, encourage accountability, and empower people to define success on their own terms.

Strength Through Resilience

My perspective is informed by resilience — not as a talking point, but as a lived reality. Experiences such as surviving domestic violence and raising a family while continuing my education have shaped my ability to connect deeply with those facing similar challenges.

I approach advocacy and mentorship with care, professionalism, and an understanding that growth looks different for everyone.

Motherhood, Leadership, and Legacy

As a mother who raised three determined young men while pursuing continual growth, I bring a grounded, real-world perspective to my work with parents and caregivers.

I believe that with the right mindset, support, and access to resources, individuals and families can break cycles, build stability, and create lasting change.
