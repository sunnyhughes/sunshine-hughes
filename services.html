Services

My work centers on advocacy, education, and empowerment — supporting individuals, families, and communities through informed guidance, lived experience, and practical skill-building.

Peer Support & Recovery Services

Kentucky Board Certified Peer Support Specialist

I provide peer-led support grounded in lived experience, professional training, and ethical standards. My work supports individuals in recovery while aligning with organizational policies and state regulations.

  • Facilitation of individual and group peer support sessions
  • Curriculum development or adherence to established SOPs
  • Ongoing training to remain current with Kentucky statutes and regulations
  • Trauma-informed, recovery-oriented support

Family Advocacy & Court Support

20+ years of lived and professional experience

I support families navigating complex systems with clarity, compassion, and advocacy — particularly single parents and caregivers facing court involvement.

  • Support during court appearances
  • Mediation assistance involving absent parents
  • Family representation within court-related processes
  • Co-facilitation of parenting groups with local nonprofits
  • Facilitation experience with the Nurturing Parent Program (Dr. Stephen J. Bavolek)

Community Education & Engagement

I collaborate with Louisville-based organizations to deliver educational sessions addressing real-world challenges faced by low-income families and children.

  • Educational workshops (financial, mental health, educational access, etc)
  • Volunteer facilitation for community-based programs
  • Participation in focus groups to improve family services

Digital Skills & Workforce Readiness

I empower individuals with practical digital skills that increase confidence, employability, and independence.

  • Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet)
  • Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Virtual communication tools (Meet / Teams)
  • Foundational digital literacy

Mentorship & Personal Development

I provide whole-person mentorship for individuals navigating personal, professional, and life transitions.

  • Confidence and self-esteem development
  • Goal clarification and life planning
  • Job readiness and personal growth support
  • Strengths-based, empathetic guidance

Let’s Work Together

Whether you’re seeking support, collaboration, training, or digital services, I welcome opportunities to partner in ways that create meaningful impact.
